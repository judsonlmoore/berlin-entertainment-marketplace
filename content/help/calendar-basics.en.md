Open [Calendar](/marketplace/calendar) for your act or venue spaces.

## Entry states

- **Available / unavailable** — your manual marks
- **Requested / tentative hold** — pipeline holds while a booking is open
- **Confirmed** — blocks both sides after dual signature

Overlapping **confirmed** bookings on the same resource are never allowed.

## Undated matches

Profile enquiries and standing open-call applies do **not** place calendar holds until a performance window is agreed. Adding dates later creates requested holds when needed.

External calendar sync is a separate milestone and may not be live yet.
