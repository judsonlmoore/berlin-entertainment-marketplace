## For talent

Open calls appear on the **venue profile**. Dated calls show a performance window; standing calls describe an ongoing need without a fixed night. Apply from the profile or the call detail.

## For buyers

Create and manage open calls under [Profile](/profile) → your venue. Choose **Dated** (required start/end) or **Standing** (optional schedule hint). Publish when ready.

There is no separate Opportunities browse in the nav — discovery stays profile-led.

## Find free acts

From an open-call detail you manage, use **Find available acts** to open Marketplace with category (and date when the call is dated).
