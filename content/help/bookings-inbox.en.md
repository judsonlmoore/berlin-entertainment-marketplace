Open [Bookings](/marketplace/bookings) for one inbox of enquiries, applications, and direct requests.

## Pipeline filters

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Needs you | Awaiting your opt-in or reply |
| In progress | Mutual interest; contacts unlocked; negotiate terms |
| Confirmed | Both parties signed; calendars blocked |
| Lost | Passed, declined, rejected, withdrawn, expired, or cancelled |
| Done | Confirmed and the performance window has ended |

## Detail page

Each booking shows origin channel, proposal fields when relevant, terms, sandbox agreement, and deposit status. Deposit tracking is independent of confirmation — Salon never holds funds.
