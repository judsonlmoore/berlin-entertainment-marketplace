Your talent or venue profile is only visible in discovery when **published**.

## Checklist

Open [Profile](/profile). Fill required fields until the built-in QA checklist passes, then use **Publish**. Publishing is self-serve — staff do not review every profile before go-live.

While unpublished you may still search the other side, but you cannot send enquiries, applications, or direct requests.

## After publish

Edits stay live; you do not need to republish for routine changes. Use **Unpublish** to leave discovery.

## Getting started rail

The left rail checklist guides publish → search → open a result → send a first enquiry. Dismiss it when you’re done.
