## Discovery

Role-segregated search: talent sees venues; buyers see acts. Unpublished profiles never appear.

## Ways to start a match

1. **Send offer** on a venue or act page — commercial terms to accept, counter, or decline
2. **Apply** to an open call listed on that venue (one-click when possible)
3. **Direct request** from an act page (buyer) — dated proposal (legacy path; profile Send offer covers dated terms too)

All routes land in **Bookings** as one pipeline.

## Contact unlock

Preferred contacts unlock only after mutual opt-in (Accept or Counter on a profile offer / shortlist / direct-request accept). Until then values stay hidden. Sending an offer shares the sender’s engagement documents with the receiver.
