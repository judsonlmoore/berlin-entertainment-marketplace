## Discovery

Role-segregated search: talent sees venues; buyers see acts. Unpublished profiles never appear.

## Ways to start a match

1. **Submit my profile** on a venue page (talent) — undated enquiry; venue taps Interested or Pass
2. **Apply** to an open call listed on that venue (one-click when possible)
3. **Direct request** from an act page (buyer) — dated proposal

All routes land in **Bookings** as one pipeline.

## Contact unlock

Preferred contacts unlock only after mutual interest (Interested / shortlist / accept). Until then values stay hidden.
