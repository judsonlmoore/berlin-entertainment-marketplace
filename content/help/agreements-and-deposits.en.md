## Terms then agreement

In a booking detail, propose and accept **terms**, then generate the **sandbox agreement**. Both designated signers must sign the current version before confirmation.

**German text is controlling.** English is a labeled convenience translation.

## Sandbox only

Current e-sign is sandbox — not a production legal signature provider. Treat it as the product workflow, not a live notary claim.

## Deposits

Deposit status is tracked on the booking and is **independent** of confirmation. Salon **never holds or escrows funds**.
