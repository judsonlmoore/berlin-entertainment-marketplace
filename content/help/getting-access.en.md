## Sign in

Use [Sign in](/sign-in) with the configured providers. After the first sign-in you choose **Talent** or **Buyer** (one role per account) and accept terms and privacy.

## Publish before you contact

You can explore the marketplace after signup, but **contact workflows** (submit a profile, apply to an open call, or send a direct request) require a **published** profile on your side.

Publishing is self-serve: complete the built-in checklist on your profile, then publish. Staff do not approve accounts before you enter the marketplace; they may still suspend abusive accounts or publication.

## Languages

Salon’s interface is available in **English** and **Deutsch**. Switch language from Account settings after you sign in, or from the header when browsing public pages.

## Need help joining?

[Contact support](/contact) if something blocks signup or role selection.
