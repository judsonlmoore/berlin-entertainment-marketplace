Salon is a **private B2B booking circle** for Berlin venues and small-format entertainers — not a consumer event platform, social network, or public directory.

## Who it’s for

- **Buyers (venues)** looking for live acts that fit a room, audience, and production setup
- **Talent (acts)** looking for rooms that match their format and rate card

Each account holds **one role** (talent or buyer). Agency multi-talent support is coming later.

## What you can do

Once you’re in and your profile is published, you can discover the other side, open a match (enquiry, open-call application, or direct request), unlock contacts only after mutual interest, agree terms, sign a sandbox agreement, and protect dates on the calendar.

## What Salon is not

- A public events listing or ticket shop
- An in-app chat product
- A payment or escrow custodian
