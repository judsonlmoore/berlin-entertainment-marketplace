## Personal Data We Collect

### Account and Authentication Data

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, authentication provider information, and marketplace role selection (entertainer/venue operator).

### Profile Information

Approved members provide venue or entertainer profile data including descriptions, locations, capacity, production resources, pricing, contact methods, portfolio items, and technical requirements.

### Booking and Transaction Data

We record applications, direct requests, agreed terms, calendar availability, hold status, agreement generation metadata, signature events, deposit status records, and audit trails of booking lifecycle changes.

### Technical and Usage Data

We automatically collect IP addresses, browser type, device information, cookies, session data, and usage analytics to operate and improve the service.

## Legal Basis for Processing

- **Performance of Contract** — Processing is necessary to provide the marketplace services you've requested.
- **Legitimate Interests** — We process data to prevent fraud, ensure platform security, and improve our services.
- **Consent** — For optional analytics and marketing cookies, we rely on your explicit consent.
- **Legal Obligation** — We process data to comply with legal requirements, including tax and financial record-keeping obligations.

## How We Use Your Data

- Provide and maintain the Salon marketplace platform
- Manage accounts, approval states, and role-based access control
- Facilitate bookings, calendar coordination, and contact unlocking
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
- Improve platform features and user experience
- Communicate service updates, booking notifications, and operational messages

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

- **Approved Members** — Role-segregated discovery surfaces share approved profile data with other approved members. Contact details are revealed only after shortlist or accepted direct request.
- **Service Providers** — We engage sub-processors to provide hosting, database, authentication, file storage, and operational services under data processing agreements.
- **Legal Requirements** — We may disclose data to comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety.

View our complete list of sub-processors at [Sub-processors List](/sub-processors)

## International Data Transfers

Salon operates with infrastructure providers based in the United States and Europe. Data transfers outside the European Economic Area (EEA) are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or equivalent safeguards as required by GDPR Article 46.

## Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Approved profiles and booking records are retained for operational and legal record-keeping purposes. Upon account closure, we will delete or anonymize data in accordance with applicable retention schedules and legal requirements.

## Your Data Protection Rights

- **Right to Access** — Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification** — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to Erasure** — Request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
- **Right to Restriction** — Request limitation of processing under certain conditions.
- **Right to Data Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object** — Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent** — Withdraw consent for processing based on consent at any time.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint** — File a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@moorewwe.com. We will respond within one month of receiving a valid request.
