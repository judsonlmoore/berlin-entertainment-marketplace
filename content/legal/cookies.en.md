## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, authenticate your identity, and understand how you use the service.

## How We Use Cookies

Salon currently uses cookies for essential functionality and security. Optional analytics and marketing cookies are not enabled. You can review preferences through our cookie consent banner.

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the platform to function. They cannot be disabled as they enable core features like authentication and security.

- **cc_cookie** — Stores your cookie consent preferences
- **authjs.session-token** — Maintains your authenticated session
- **authjs.csrf-token** — Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks

### Analytics Cookies

Salon does not currently set analytics cookies. If a privacy-reviewed analytics provider is added later, this page will list those cookies and consent will be required first.

### Marketing Cookies

Salon does not currently set marketing cookies.
