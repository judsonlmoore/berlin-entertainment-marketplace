## Introduction

This page lists all third-party sub-processors engaged by Moore World Wide Enterprises, LLC to process personal data on behalf of Salon marketplace users. We carefully select sub-processors that maintain high standards of data protection and security.

## Current Sub-processors

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data Location | Data Protection Safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vercel Inc.** ([vercel.com](https://vercel.com)) | Hosting, CDN, serverless functions, edge computing, and file storage infrastructure | United States and global edge network | Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR-compliant DPA |
| **Neon (Neon Tech Inc.)** ([neon.tech](https://neon.tech)) | Serverless PostgreSQL database hosting and management | United States and EU regions (configurable) | Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), SOC 2 Type II, GDPR-compliant DPA |
| **Google LLC** ([google.com](https://google.com)) | Optional analytics (Google Analytics) and authentication provider (Google OAuth) when enabled | United States and global | EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses, GDPR-compliant DPA |
| **Microsoft Corporation** ([microsoft.com](https://microsoft.com)) | Optional analytics (Microsoft Clarity) when enabled | United States and EU | EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Standard Contractual Clauses, ISO 27001, SOC 2 |
| **GitHub, Inc.** ([github.com](https://github.com)) | Authentication provider (GitHub OAuth) when enabled | United States | Standard Contractual Clauses, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR-compliant practices |

## Changes to Sub-processors

We will notify users at least 30 days in advance of adding or replacing sub-processors. Notification will be provided via email and a notice on this page. Users may object to a new sub-processor within 30 days of notification.
